# Ambro Connects — content and product plan

## Product promise

Ambro Connects is a member network by Hamras, known as Ambro, and Mandarin Roots. It helps people visiting China discover people who live and do business there, assess their experience, and request a useful introduction.

Use **Ambro Connect China** for China-based members and **Ambro Connect Global** for visitors and international members. The product is **Ambro Connects**. Avoid switching the Global membership name to “World.”

The first release should feel like a social discovery app: real people, useful profiles, project stories, saved members, and considered introductions. It must not imply that a membership guarantees business, a visa, travel availability, or a successful transaction.

## Membership model

| Membership | Who it serves | Price and access | Activation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ambro Connect China | People who reside and do business in China | RMB 1,000 for 12 months | Profile review and payment confirmation |
| Ambro Connect Global | People exploring business, travel, sourcing, or opportunities in China | RMB 100 per month | Payment confirmation |
| Ambro Connect Global — trip benefit | People who join an eligible Mandarin Roots China trip with Ambro | First 12 months complimentary | Trip booking verified by the team |

Prices must show RMB consistently; do not silently convert them. A complimentary year is an entitlement, not a promise of automatic future billing. Show the access end date after activation. Do not claim automatic renewal until billing and cancellation rules have been selected and implemented.

## Information architecture

**Public:** Home · Discover preview · How it works · Memberships · About Ambro · Sign in / Join.

**Member app:** Discover · Saved · Connections · Account. China members also have Edit profile and Portfolio. Global members have travel/business interests. A separate review view serves authorized staff.

**Supporting pages:** Membership status · Community standards · Privacy · Terms · Report a concern · Help. Publish actual operator/contact and policy details before production membership collection.

## Discovery and profile content

Search placeholder: **Try a city, service, or specialty**

Category chips: All · Sourcing · Logistics · Translation · Business services · Travel & local support. Categories describe services, not vetting or professional certification.

Filter fields: City · Specialty · Languages · Availability. Selected filters stay visible and can be cleared individually. Search and filters must change the actual result set.

Each member card includes photo, display name, city, role/business, short introduction, up to three specialties, languages, save action, and **View profile**. A verification badge must identify its meaning; membership payment alone is not business verification.

Profile sections:

- **Introduction:** who the member is, where they work, languages, and who they can help.
- **Expertise:** services, industries, cities served, and availability.
- **Work & achievements:** dated project stories, photos, member-owned links, and clearly attributed accomplishments. Show “Member provided” until reviewed.
- **Reviews:** genuine feedback from eligible completed connections. Display “No reviews yet” and no numeric rating when empty.
- **Connect:** **Tell [name] what you have in mind.** Request fields: purpose, approximate timing, city, and a short message. State **Your contact details stay private until your request is accepted.**

Do not scrape or publish anyone’s photo, business details, contact details, or achievements without an appropriate basis and permission.

## Joining and access journeys

### Global

Choose Global → create account / sign in → name, home country, interests, intended China city → choose monthly membership or trip benefit → submit the relevant request → see honest status → discover and save → request a connection after activation.

For the trip benefit, collect only a booking reference and booking email initially. Staff checks the booking in a trusted source. Avoid requesting passport images or travel documents in general profile forms.

### China

Choose China → create account / sign in → China city, business/role, languages, specialties, introduction → add photo and optional portfolio → agree to profile visibility → submit application → team review → payment confirmation → profile becomes discoverable.

Allow members to save a draft and return. A submission does not publicly publish an unreviewed profile. Keep required fields limited; portfolio and achievements can be added later.

### Required status language

| State | Member-facing copy | Capability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft | “Your profile is saved. Finish it when you’re ready.” | Edit own draft |
| Submitted | “Your application is with the Ambro Connects team.” | View status; edit requested details |
| Trip review | “We’re checking your trip booking. Your complimentary year starts after approval.” | View status; correct booking reference |
| Payment pending | “Your membership is awaiting payment confirmation.” | View request status; no paid access claim |
| Changes requested | “A few details need your attention before we can continue.” | View specific private feedback; resubmit |
| Active | “You’re connected. Your membership is active until [date].” | Member access according to role |
| Expired | “Your membership ended on [date]. Renew to keep making connections.” | Account, saved data, renewal request |

Until a payment provider is selected, present **Request membership** / **Submit for review**, not a functioning checkout. Explicitly say **Payments are not collected on this website yet. The team will provide the next step after review.** Do not capture card details, fabricate a successful payment, or activate access from a client-side toggle.

## Connections, reviews, and safety

Connection requests move through Pending → Accepted or Declined → Completed. Show actions and meaningful empty states: **No connections yet. Find someone who fits your plans and send an introduction.** A declined request does not disclose a reason unless the recipient chooses to share one.

Members may review only an accepted connection marked completed, once per reviewer per connection. Prevent self-reviews and rating manipulation. Keep review text and rating linked to the eligible connection and moderation status. Completion is not proof of financial success. Report and block actions must be available; blocked members must not continue sending requests.

Collect only information needed for the service. Public profiles should never expose email, phone, booking references, private request messages, or billing data. Enforce privacy, role, ownership, and membership permissions on the server/database. Explain how to change visibility, withdraw a pending application, and request deletion. An admin decision needs a private audit trail.

## Design direction — after content

Use a warm, editorial social-app direction: ivory surfaces, deep ink text, vermillion actions, human photography, and generous space. Avoid a generic travel booking page or a dashboard dominated by metrics.

Suggested tokens: page `#F7F3EA`; surface `#FFFFFF`; text `#182421`; secondary `#54635D`; border `#DADFD7`; action `#A93124`; soft accent `#F6E6DA`. Verify contrast in rendered combinations. Pair an expressive display serif sparingly with a clear sans serif for navigation, profiles, forms, and small text. Do not use decorative Chinese characters as filler.

**Mobile first:** 360 px and up; single-column cards; horizontally scrollable category chips with no clipped labels; a compact search header; bottom navigation inside the member app; persistent safe-area spacing; one primary action per screen. Keep forms in short steps and preserve values when users go back. On desktop, widen to a discovery grid with a secondary filter rail and generous reading widths.

**Hierarchy:** an expressive headline, clear membership split, and large member photos. Profile content should remain readable without hovering. Use consistent card radii and restrained borders; avoid excessive glass effects, gradients, or ornamental animation.

**Accessibility:** semantic landmarks and headings; visible keyboard focus; explicit labels; 44 px minimum touch targets; correct button/link semantics; error messages linked to fields; status announcements; meaningful alt text; reduced-motion support; no color-only statuses. Modals trap focus and restore it on close. A filter sheet must be usable by keyboard and screen reader.

**Motion:** short, subtle feedback for saving, filters, navigation, and expanded content. Avoid auto-playing carousels and motion that delays access.

## Delivery and decisions

Build an independent Next.js app in `apps/ambro-connects`, with a separate Vercel project. Request `ambroconnects.vercel.app` if available; `.vercel.com` is not the standard deployment suffix. Keep the Mandarin Roots application working. Database project/schema choice is pending and must preserve isolation and existing data.

First reviewable release: polished landing page; responsive discovery; explicitly labeled demonstration profiles; working search/filter/save/profile interactions; role-aware membership forms; truthful application states; accessible navigation. Persisted accounts, membership enforcement, staff review, requests, and review eligibility must use a real protected backend before being described as operational.

Before paid launch, settle: operator and support details; payment provider and settlement currency; renewal/cancellation/refund rules; eligible trip criteria and entitlement start date; China member verification evidence and review ownership; moderation/report response process; profile visibility and data retention; membership expiry behavior; actual Hamras imagery and approved biography. These decisions do not block building the reviewable experience, but they must not be replaced by invented promises.
